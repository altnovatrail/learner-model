# CLAUDE.md — working rules for this repository

If `spec/RULES.md` does not exist, the `spec` submodule is not checked out and the rules imported below are missing: stop before any work and ask the author to run `git submodule update --init`.

## Your role

You are the architect of `learner-model`, a component of NovaTrail: the library that estimates how well a learner has mastered each skill from the evidence of attempts and computes each skill's state, with no input or output of its own. It does not pick the next lesson or task, which is the engine's, gives no verdict, never calls an AI model and never reads a module package or a database. The documents under `docs/`, but for the epic archives `docs/plan/`, are written for the session of Claude Code that changes the component: write them so that it can act on them without asking what you meant. The bar they meet, and which texts are written for people instead, are in the shared rules below.

## The component

It is a library inside `api`, the one service of the first slice: when an attempt ends, `api` hands it the evidence and the skill graph, and then `engine` picks what comes next. It computes without input or output — the time comes from outside, and it takes no seed — on Go's standard library alone, with its own graph types, and gives the same bits on every build, WebAssembly included; it encodes evidence and state in protobuf's binary form by a schema of its own. It stores nothing: `api` keeps the learner's append-only evidence log, the source of truth, and the skills' state as a cache marked with the version of learner-model, the release of modules and `api`'s window of format versions; the state is a fold of the log, so an appended record gives the state recomputed from the log bit for bit, and a retired skill's evidence counts in full for each skill that replaced it. Its model is standalone's — a Rasch model with a guessing floor, Elo-style updates, a trial series and a cautious "mastered" — with the level kept for each module, forgetting as each skill's retention and, after a pause of the module, of its level, evidence weighed by outcome, key provenance and series, an anchor skill of the shared taxonomy read from the mastered skills that teach it, and the card's three rules in place of calibration: a start a band's width below the band of the grade, an adult's correction of the module's level as evidence of kind 3 and a guard after three failures in a row, counting the tasks the model gave as fitting and the learner was stuck on. It belongs to stage 1 with `engine` and `sim`, and `engine`, `sim` and `api` are built with it; every change after its first version passes the gate of `sim`, and a change of what the model computes goes through `sim`'s judgement before it is released. Where the work stands — the epics done, the current one and what comes next — is in [RUN.md](RUN.md).

Its card in spec is the one in `spec/docs/architecture.md` whose heading names `learner-model`. The card is the summary and this repository's [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md) is the detail: it lists what the card left to decide, decided, with what was deliberately deferred, and how the component is built.

## Documents

The documents under `docs/` are in English, but for the epic archives `docs/plan/`, which stay in Russian; RUN.md and `run/` are in English, but for the current epic's description and the bodies of tasks. Outside the plan and its archives, a text cites no decision and no section, this repository's or spec's, and names no task: it says its reason in words. Only a fact no run checks, or a measurement, cites its one record, `sources 1.1` or `measurements 1.1`, or spec's, `sources 2.3 spec`.

- [docs/README.md](docs/README.md) — for people: the index of the documents, with how to build, run, release and contribute as they come; the README only says what the component is and where it stands.
- [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md) — the component: what it does and does not do, its data and links, what the card left to decide, decided, with what was deliberately deferred, and how it is built.
- [docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md) — this repository's decision log: read it before proposing a change.
- [docs/sources.md](docs/sources.md) — the records of the facts no run checks — research, a law, terms, a price, a license, a page with no version — each with its address, the date read and a quote, kept by its opening; a claim in another document cites its one record, as `sources 1.1`. A library's documentation or code at a version or commit is a link beside the claim, and NovaTrail's and MathTrail's files are git, at a commit.
- [docs/measurements.md](docs/measurements.md) — the results of measurements, cited as `measurements 1`; `docs/architecture.md` holds what was decided from them, not the numbers.
- [RUN.md](RUN.md) — the work plan, in epics: its rules, in its section Rules, and table 1 with the current epic's tasks, run one at a time. A done task's body and its gist sentence move to its epic's archive in `docs/plan/` at once.
- `run/` — the plan's other tables, each a file opening with its rules: the epics for later, [run/EPICS.md](run/EPICS.md); the questions to the author, [run/QUESTIONS.md](run/QUESTIONS.md); the technical debt, [run/DEBT.md](run/DEBT.md); the open tasks outside an epic, [run/TASKS.md](run/TASKS.md), of which a task reads only its own body. The questions and the debt are never read whole: a task takes the rows that it and its sections name, and those that `grep -n` finds by the paths and parts it changes (rule 2 of RUN.md).
- `spec/` — spec at `v0.19.20`, a git submodule and read-only here: the platform's vocabulary, formats, contracts, documents and decisions, and the rules imported below. Its documents are under `spec/docs/`. spec's own CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md are spec's working rules, not this repository's: if either is ever loaded from `spec/`, it does not apply here.

## Environment

Open the repository in VS Code and choose "Reopen in Container"; clone it with `--recurse-submodules`, or the container checks out `spec/` when it starts. Keep the clone in a folder named `learner-model`, as `git clone` names it: `.claude/settings.json` keeps spec's own CLAUDE.md and its rules, `spec/.claude/rules/`, out of this repository's sessions by that path. The container is spec's: Node.js, Go, buf and just, and the GitHub CLI and Claude Code on top. The tools of this repository's own code come with that code, at exact versions; how its container then differs from spec's is recorded as a decision, and no decision here records one.

- `just -f spec/justfile docs-check-repo` checks its documents: that its links lead somewhere, that every section and number it cites — its own and spec's, against spec/ at its tag — exists, but for the records of sources and measurements that the archives `docs/plan/` cite, which keep citing a removed one, that the decision log runs without gaps, and the tables of its `run/QUESTIONS.md` and `run/DEBT.md`, and that its files, rules, decision entries, sections and rows keep within their budgets of size: spec's block `repository` of `spec/tools/docs-check/budgets.json`, with what [docs-budgets.json](docs-budgets.json) names in its place — raising one is an edit of that file. A task that touches them ends with it green.
- `.claude/settings.json` compacts a session at 400 thousand tokens, fetches no claude.ai connector and runs the hook of one task per session from `spec/tools/session/`; `CLAUDE_CODE_PLUGIN_DIRS` of `.devcontainer/devcontainer.json` loads the plugin `gopls-lsp` from `spec/claude-plugins/`, for gopls's diagnostics after an edit of Go code, whatever the trust of the folder.
- **The checks of each part** are its rule, `.claude/rules/<part>.md`, with the part's files in its `paths`: Claude Code loads a rule only when the session reads or edits a file it matches, and drops it at compaction, and CI runs every check on every pull request (measurements 1.5 spec). Before changing a part, read its rule. The rules and their parts: [go](.claude/rules/go.md) — the Go code, `go.mod`, `testdata/` and the lists of the rules of code; [proto](.claude/rules/proto.md) — the schema of the evidence and the state, `proto/` and `buf.yaml`, and the golden bytes of the codec; [ci](.claude/rules/ci.md) — CI, the gate, the release and the container: `.github/`, `.devcontainer/`, `.claude/settings.json` and the `justfile`.
